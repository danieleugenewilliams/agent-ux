# Robot Battle: the outcome

*Draft by Claude for Daniel to rewrite in his own words. The loop reads this file; only
Daniel edits it.*

Robot Battle is a game my two sons want to play, and while they play it, they learn their
multiplication facts by heart. Their mom asked for the math so the game reinforces the
lessons they're already doing at home. The goal is every single-digit fact, 1×1 through
9×9, answered correctly in under three seconds. The fight comes first: the math is how you
repair a robot, not a quiz in the way of the game.

## How we'd know

- Each boy's progress grid shows every fact Mastered: right in under three seconds on three
  different days.
- They choose to play without being asked, most days, for a few minutes at a time.
- A fact they miss comes back soon, and a fact they know stops taking up their time.
- A parent can see, in under a minute, which facts are slow and how much they've practiced
  this week.
- It works the way they actually play: a phone held sideways, a keyboard, or a gamepad.

## Not the goal (for now)

- Facts beyond 9×9, and division. They use regrouping for bigger numbers.
- Physics. It may come later; nothing here should block it, and nothing should claim it.
- Anything that leaves the browser: no accounts, no servers, no names sent anywhere.
